## Interpretation / next gate
Both models produced usable patches within scope without repair. This is evidence for bounded delegation only, not proof of cost savings or equal capability: tasks differed, sample size is one per model, and no direct-implementation baseline was measured. Terra's unwired foundation deliberately avoids integration risk; Luna's small change does not establish ability to handle cross-site state/auth. Next trial should use similarly sized consumer migrations with timestamped preparation/submission/review boundaries. Continue bounded work; do not delegate the remaining plan wholesale. A substantial repair or repeated contract misunderstanding should trigger reassessment.
